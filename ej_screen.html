Map interpretation

The percent proximity to a Superfund site is highest at the northern end of Monterey county, which is also where there is the highest percentage of people of color. The areas closest to a Superfund site are consistent with the location of Fort Ord, a former U.S Army base that was established as a Superfund site by the EPA in 1990. These maps display that people of color may be more exposed to Fort Ord’s contaminated soil, groundwater, and unexploded ordinances due to their close proximity to the Superfund site.
